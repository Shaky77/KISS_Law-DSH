## 1. 安's original words (verbatim · 逐字)

> 「R 在地球这个尺度上，可能是一个**完备集**，但是在宇宙尺度上，可能就被**降级成了一个 S 点**，因为宇宙中还有黑洞等等的存在。而 **S 不是完备集**，是一直在**追着 R 做验证**的科学集。S 的点到点之间是**直线**，在短链里，甚至比 R 这个长链看起来**更准确、更真**，因为太贴合了。但是 **R 是长链**，也就是之前提到过的 **1°∠**。在美术中，**没有曲线的画法，全是画的直线**，之所以图画里有曲线，**全部都由直线切出来的**。」

**EN paraphrase.** On the scale of Earth, R may be a *complete set*; but on the scale of the universe it is downgraded to a single *S point* (black holes, etc.). S is *not* a complete set — it is the scientific set that *chases R to verify it*. The segments between S points are *straight lines*; on a short chain, S looks *more accurate and more true* than the long R chain, because it fits so tightly. But R is the *long chain* — the 1°-angle we spoke of. In drawing, there is no way to draw a curve except by straight segments; every curve is cut out of straight segments.
